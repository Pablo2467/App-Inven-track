
# 00 — Definition of Ready

A story does not enter sprint planning until every applicable criterion below is
satisfied.

## Checklist

| # | Criterion | How it is verified |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The story has a clear and testable objective | The story contains a concise objective and acceptance criteria |
| 2 | Acceptance criteria are written in a testable form | Given/When/Then or another explicit verification format |
| 3 | The story fits within one sprint | Estimated at 8 points or less |
| 4 | Dependencies are identified | Dependencies are documented in the backlog |
| 5 | The affected module is identified | Checked against [`../09-modules/module-catalog.md`](../09-modules/module-catalog.md) |
| 6 | UX information is available when a screen changes | Wireframe or design reference is attached |
| 7 | No unresolved business question blocks implementation | Product decisions are documented before planning |
| 8 | The requested behavior is within the approved scope | Checked against [`../01-context/scope.md`](../01-context/scope.md) |

## What "Ready" is not

Ready does not mean that the implementation has already been designed in full
detail.

It means the team understands what must be delivered and can begin development
without stopping to resolve an essential business question.
