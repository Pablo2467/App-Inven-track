# 03 — Vision

## Vision statement

For **businesses that need to control their inventory**, who need to **record product movements accurately and apply FIFO consistently**, **Iven-Track** is a **single-deployable inventory management application** that **centralizes product information and automates stock control**, unlike **manual records and spreadsheets**, which **can become difficult to keep accurate and traceable**.

## Objectives

| # | Objective | Metric |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ensure correct FIFO processing | 100% of stock exits follow FIFO for the corresponding product |
| 2 | Centralize inventory movements | 100% of registered stock entries and exits are stored in Iven-Track |
| 3 | Maintain accurate available stock | Available quantity is calculated from the remaining quantities of recorded stock entries |
| 4 | Improve inventory traceability | Every stock movement can be associated with a product and its movement date |

## Reviewing the vision

The vision should be revisited when the business scope changes significantly or when a new major inventory capability is proposed.

Any new objective should remain consistent with the product scope defined in
[`../01-context/scope.md`](../01-context/scope.md).
